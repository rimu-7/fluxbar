Install FluxBar

1. Drag FluxBar.app onto Applications in this disk image.
2. Eject the disk image.
3. Open FluxBar from Applications. Look for its clipboard and speed icons in the menu bar.

Everything is set up inside the app. Terminal and the curl installer are not required.

Battery limit: open Battery, choose Enable if shown, and approve the macOS administrator
dialog. If Update is shown after upgrading FluxBar, choose it to update battery controls.
On supported Macs that use the macOS charge limit, no helper setup is needed.
Charging capabilities and available limits depend on your Mac and macOS version.

Start at login: open FluxBar Settings and turn on Start at Login.
Update: open Settings and choose Check Now, or replace FluxBar using a newer DMG.
Caffeine: open the Caffeine tab after Speed and turn on Keep awake.

If macOS blocks an unnotarized release, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and
choose Open Anyway for FluxBar. Apple-signed, notarized releases do not need this step.

To remove FluxBar, first turn off the charge limit and choose Remove Battery Helper
if shown in Settings. Turn off Start at Login, quit FluxBar, then move it to Trash.

Help and releases: https://github.com/rimu-7/fluxbar/releases
